sub_080A47A0: in-tree draft (register-asm heavy) is 86 diff lines / 524 bytes. A clean, asm-free
  rewrite is in shard108_sub_080A47A0_clean.c (164 lines / 512 bytes). Facts learned:
  - The "frame" is NOT a struct: tile, rotation, sx, sy are separate address-taken u16/s16 locals
    (slots 0x10..0x16 in address-taken order: &tile/&rotation (animate call) must be taken before
    &sx/&sy, so do not hoist px=&sx in source); metadata (0x18), x (0x1C), y (0x20) are spilled
    pseudos -> that is why ROM reloads x/y from the stack instead of forwarding the stored value.
  - Wait loop is `wait: if (frame == 0) { sub_080003F0(); goto wait; }` and the else branch reads
    the global again (`animation = frame10847`, not a cached local) -> ROM's second literal load.
  - Outer loop is for(;;) with `if (CED8 || CB78 || CD00) break;` at the bottom.
  Remaining in the clean version: ROM spills x/y and keeps &sx/&sy in sl/r9 (ours keeps x/y in
  r7/r8 and spills &sx); styles-0x1C computed as reg-28 into ip; &side reused in r6.
  Permuter 5 min on the clean version: only invalid results (hoists object->x out of the loop).
