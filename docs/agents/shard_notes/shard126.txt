    expand_end_loop rotates everything up to the last exit jump (the break), and
    duplicate_loop_exit_test copies the match test and the 40-byte struct copy ahead of the
    loop, which accounts for the duplicated ldmia/stmia blocks and the 0x1D0 spill.
  - gUnk_03003170 through struct TileSys fields: base CSE plus `adds #140/#144`, not
    sym+off literals.

  - switch-7 cases 2/7 and the f20 packet-record switch (switch 16): goto tails
